# Product

The canonical product brief lives at [ui/PRODUCT.md](ui/PRODUCT.md). This root context keeps development tools aligned without moving design documentation out of `ui/`.

## Register

product

## Users

People who browse, share, discuss, and build relationships around daily life and interests across mobile and desktop.

## Product Purpose

Combine Xiaohongshu-style discovery efficiency with X-style people, context, and discussion. Sharing comes first, then social connection, continued participation, and an extensible community ecosystem.

## Brand Personality

Sincere, lively, and able to grow.

## Anti-references

Not a dating app, autoplay short-video product, SaaS dashboard, game-like reward system, or a direct visual copy of Xiaohongshu or X.

## Design Principles

1. Content before containers.
2. Discovery and relationships carry equal weight.
3. Never lose author, source, time, or discussion context.
4. Unavailable capabilities must be explicit and never imitate success.
5. Familiar interaction patterns beat decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA, support keyboard use, 44px touch targets, reduced motion, Chinese user-generated content, and varied media ratios.
